# mdview extensions

Examples for behaviour beyond the GFM spec (GitHub rendering details that
mdview reproduces). Run with `mdview_test spec tests/spec/mdview-extensions.txt --strict`.

## Front matter

YAML front matter at the very top of a file is shown as a table of the
top-level keys, like GitHub does.

```````````````````````````````` example
---
title: Hello
layout: post
---

# Hi
.
<table><thead><tr><th>title</th><th>layout</th></tr></thead>
<tbody><tr><td>Hello</td><td>post</td></tr></tbody></table>
<h1>Hi</h1>
````````````````````````````````

Quoted strings keep their colons; flow sequences become a one-row table.

```````````````````````````````` example
---
title: "Part 2: the sequel"
tags: [a, 'b, c', d]
---
.
<table><thead><tr><th>title</th><th>tags</th></tr></thead>
<tbody><tr><td>Part 2: the sequel</td><td><table><tbody><tr><td>a</td><td>b, c</td><td>d</td></tr></tbody></table></td></tr></tbody></table>
````````````````````````````````

Block sequences, nested mappings, comments and empty values.

```````````````````````````````` example
---
# a comment
authors:
  - name: Ann
    role: dev
  - Bob
meta:
  draft: true
empty:
---
.
<table><thead><tr><th>authors</th><th>meta</th><th>empty</th></tr></thead>
<tbody><tr><td><table><tbody><tr><td><table><thead><tr><th>name</th><th>role</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Ann</td><td>dev</td></tr></tbody></table></td><td>Bob</td></tr></tbody></table></td><td><table><thead><tr><th>draft</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>true</td></tr></tbody></table></td><td></td></tr></tbody></table>
````````````````````````````````

A sequence at the same indentation as its key.

```````````````````````````````` example
---
list:
- one
- two
---
.
<table><thead><tr><th>list</th></tr></thead>
<tbody><tr><td><table><tbody><tr><td>one</td><td>two</td></tr></tbody></table></td></tr></tbody></table>
````````````````````````````````

Block scalars: literal keeps line breaks, folded joins lines.

```````````````````````````````` example
---
literal: |
  line one
  line two
folded: >
  joined
  words
---
.
<table><thead><tr><th>literal</th><th>folded</th></tr></thead>
<tbody><tr><td>line one<br />line two</td><td>joined words</td></tr></tbody></table>
````````````````````````````````

Special characters are text, not HTML.

```````````````````````````````` example
---
formula: a < b & c > d
---
.
<table><thead><tr><th>formula</th></tr></thead>
<tbody><tr><td>a &lt; b &amp; c &gt; d</td></tr></tbody></table>
````````````````````````````````

YAML beyond the supported subset is shown as a code block.

```````````````````````````````` example
---
base: &anchor
  x: 1
copy: *anchor
---
.
<pre><code class="language-yaml">base: &amp;anchor
  x: 1
copy: *anchor
</code></pre>
````````````````````````````````

Without a closing delimiter it is ordinary Markdown.

```````````````````````````````` example
---
title: x
.
<hr />
<p>title: x</p>
````````````````````````````````

Only at the very start of the file.

```````````````````````````````` example
Text

---
title: x
---
.
<p>Text</p>
<hr />
<h2>title: x</h2>
````````````````````````````````

An empty block, or one that doesn't start like a mapping, is left to Markdown
(the GFM spec renders these as thematic breaks and setext headings).

```````````````````````````````` example
---
---
Text
.
<hr />
<hr />
<p>Text</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
---
Foo
---
Bar
.
<hr />
<h2>Foo</h2>
<p>Bar</p>
````````````````````````````````

Four dashes are a thematic break, not front matter.

```````````````````````````````` example
----
title: x
---
.
<hr />
<h2>title: x</h2>
````````````````````````````````
